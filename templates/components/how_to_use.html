How to Use SaveVibe

1. Copy Video URL

Copy the YouTube video link from the browser or YouTube app.

2. Paste the URL

Paste the copied link into the SaveVibe URL input field.

3. Choose Format

Select your preferred format.

MP4 Video MP3 Audio

4. Choose Quality

Select the quality you want. Available quality depends on the original video.

144p 360p 480p 720p HD 1080p Full HD

5. Download Instantly

Click Download and save the file to your device. Downloads begin immediately after processing.
